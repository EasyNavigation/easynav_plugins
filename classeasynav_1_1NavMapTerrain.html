Detailed Description

Places a planar (x, y, yaw) estimate on the surface of a NavMap.

The height is the surface's under (x, y), following the surface the robot is on (a level of a multi-level map is kept while it continues). Roll and pitch are the IMU's when given, the surface's otherwise; yaw is kept.
