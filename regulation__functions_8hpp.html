Detailed Description

Pure regulation heuristics for the Regulated Pure Pursuit controller.

These are the same regulation terms described in: S. Macenski, S. Singh, F. Martin, J. Gines, "Regulated Pure Pursuit for Robot Path Tracking", Autonomous Robots, 2023 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20026), ported from nav2_regulated_pure_pursuit_controller. Since EasyNav controllers have no costmap available, the cost-based regulation term is replaced by an equivalent one driven by the distance to the nearest point in the fused point-cloud perception (see RegulatedPurePursuitController).
