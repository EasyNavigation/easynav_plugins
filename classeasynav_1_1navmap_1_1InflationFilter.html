Detailed Description

"inflated_obstacles": cost by distance (along the surface) to the obstacles of the "obstacles" layer and, optionally, to the open edges of the NavMap.

Incremental: an exact distance field (navmap::DistanceField) is updated with the obstacle cells that appear or disappear, and only the cells whose distance or obstacle value changed are rewritten (and recorded as changed). A new map or obstacle layer starts it over.
