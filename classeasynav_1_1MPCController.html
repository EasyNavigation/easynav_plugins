Detailed Description

Model predictive controller: tracks the path with a linear time-varying MPC.

Each cycle samples a reference along the path ahead (at the speed its curvature, the goal and the obstacles allow), linearizes the motion model (diff, omni, ackermann or aerial) around the previous solution and solves a QP over the horizon with OSQP, with the robot limits as constraints and half-planes keeping the robot's shape off the perceived obstacles.
