Detailed Description

The MPC's parts: motion models and their linearization, the QP solver, the robot's shape against obstacles, and the reference along the path.

Enumeration Type Documentation

◆ MotionModel

enum class MotionModel
strong

How the robot moves.

The pose is (x, y, yaw), or (x, y, z, yaw) for Aerial; the velocity is in the body frame: Diff and Ackermann (vx, wz), Omni (vx, vy, wz), Aerial (vx, vy, vz, wz). Ackermann moves as Diff (a kinematic bicycle at the rear axle); its turning radius is a constraint, not the model.
