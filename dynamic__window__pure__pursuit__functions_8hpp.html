Detailed Description

Dynamic Window Pure Pursuit (DWPP) helper functions, ported from nav2_regulated_pure_pursuit_controller.

These are plain math functions over velocities and accelerations, so they carry over to EasyNav unchanged.

Fumiya Ohnishi and Masaki Takahashi, "DWPP: Dynamic Window Pure Pursuit Considering Velocity and Acceleration Constraints", arXiv:2601.15006, 2026.
