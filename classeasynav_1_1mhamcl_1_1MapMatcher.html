Detailed Description

Finds the poses of the map from which the last perception could have been obtained.

The map is stored in a pyramid of resolutions, each level halving the previous one. The search starts at the coarsest level, evaluating every free cell at angle_step; each finer level refines the best candidates of the previous one (its 4 sub-cells, and half the angle step around their heading), keeping the best beam_width, down to the original map.

A pose scores the mean of exp(-d^2 / 2 sigma^2) over the perception points, d being the distance from each point to the nearest obstacle of the map. Each level widens sigma with its cell size and angle step, so a candidate a few centimeters or degrees off the true pose still scores well and is not lost before it is refined. The final scores (level 0) are in [0, 1].

Once built, it is immutable, so get_matches can safely run in another thread.
