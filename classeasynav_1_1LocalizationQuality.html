Detailed Description

How well the particles explain what the sensors see, in absolute terms.

Each correction gives two scores in [0, 1]: the best particle's and the mean of all particles' (the fraction of points that land on obstacles of the map). The covariance only tells how spread the particles are; a lost filter can be compact and wrong, and these scores drop when it is.

  • match_score(): the best particle's score, smoothed (fast average).
  • quality_ratio(): fast / slow average of the mean score (Augmented MCL, Thrun et al., Probabilistic Robotics, 8.3.5). Near 1 while the scores keep their usual level; well below 1 right after they drop, e.g. when the robot is kidnapped.
  • injection_probability(): max(0, 1 - fast / slow), the share of particles to replace with random ones.

Each average starts as the plain mean of the scores so far (until its alpha is smaller), so the first, still unconverged corrections do not set the level for long.
